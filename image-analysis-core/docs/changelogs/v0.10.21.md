# v0.10.21 — Irradiance normalisation fix (reflectance was ~12× too low)

## Summary

Calibrated reflectance for DJI M3M multispectral bands was coming out roughly an
order of magnitude too low (NIR median ≈ 0.011 instead of the physical ≈ 0.13;
Red ≈ 0.005 instead of ≈ 0.06). The cause was that
`calculate_reflectance_from_signal` divided the camera signal by the **raw**
`Irradiance` XMP value, but that value is a downwelling-light-sensor (DLS) reading
captured with its own exposure time and gain. It must first be normalised by
`IrradianceExposureTime × IrradianceGain` to be on the same per-unit basis as the
camera signal. The missing division left a constant ≈ 11.6× factor in the
denominator. Verified directly against the sample M3M capture in `data/`: after
the fix, per-band reflectance medians are Green 0.056 / Red 0.10 / RedEdge 0.156 /
NIR 0.189 — all physically sensible — with NDVI in the expected vegetation range.
This is the absolute-scale half of the long-running multispectral problem; v0.10.20
fixed the flight-line banding (relative, cross-band), this fixes the magnitude.

## Bug Fixes

### fix(calibration): reflectance ~12× too low — raw DLS irradiance used without normalising by its exposure/gain

**Files:**
- `services/radiometric_calibration/app/core/services/interfaces.py`
- `services/radiometric_calibration/app/core/services/mavic_m3m_calibration.py`
- `services/radiometric_calibration/app/core/services/base_calibrator.py`

**Root cause**

`calculate_reflectance_from_signal` computed
`reflectance = camera_signal × gain_adj / Irradiance` using the raw
`drone-dji:Irradiance` / `Camera:Irradiance` value (≈ 10 000–13 000). That number
is a raw DLS sensor count taken with its own integration time and gain, exposed in
XMP as `Camera:IrradianceExposureTime` (0.182 s) and `Camera:IrradianceGain` (64).
Because the camera signal is already normalised per exposure/gain but the
irradiance was not, the ratio carried an extra ≈ `0.182 × 64 ≈ 11.6` factor,
shrinking every reflectance value by that amount. Output reflectance was so small
that `np.clip(.., 0, 1)` never engaged and the error was invisible.

Measured on the `data/` sample (raw DN median ≈ 15 312 for NIR):

| Band | reflectance before | reflectance after | irradiance_raw → norm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | 0.005 | 0.056 | 13031 → 1119 |
| Red | 0.005 | 0.100 | 11913 → 1023 |
| RedEdge | 0.009 | 0.156 | 9293 → 798 |
| NIR | 0.011 | 0.189 | 10633 → 913 |

**Fix**

- Added `irradiance_exposure_time` and `irradiance_gain` fields to
  `CalibrationMetadata` (default 1.0 → no change for sensors that omit them).
- `mavic_m3m_calibration.extract_calibration_metadata` populates them from the
  `IrradianceExposureTime` / `IrradianceGain` XMP fields (already exposed by the
  v0.10.19 metadata-reader property additions).
- `calculate_reflectance_from_signal` now divides by
  `Irradiance / (IrradianceExposureTime × IrradianceGain)`.
- `_log_reflectance_scale` logs both raw and normalised irradiance for traceability.

**Impact**

Reflectance band products and vegetation indices are now on a correct physical
scale. NDVI's central tendency was already sane (the ratio partly cancels the
error), but absolute reflectance, NDRE/GNDVI thresholds, and any agronomic
interpretation that depends on reflectance magnitude were wrong before this fix.
